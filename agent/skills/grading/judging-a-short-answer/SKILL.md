# Judging a short answer

`task.json` has the question and an `answerGuide`. The guide is the standard. It usually
says what the answer must join together and what does not count, and it was written by
whoever wrote the question.

## Where the line sits

Pass an answer that contains the idea, in any words, in any order, at any length. The reader
is being tested on the idea and not on phrasing, spelling, or notation.

Fail an answer that names the symptom when the guide asks for the cause. Fail one that is
true but does not answer the question. Fail one that is right for the wrong reason where the
guide says the reason is the point.

Do not fail an answer for being terse. "Because the factors multiply and each is below one"
is a complete answer to a question about compounding.

## The reason

One or two sentences, addressed to the person. Say what the answer was missing, or, when it
passed, what it got right, in terms of the idea rather than the words.

Never write "correct!" or "good job". They can see the outcome.

## When you are unsure

An answer you cannot place against the guide is not a fail. If the guide does not settle it,
say so in the reason and pass it: a question whose answer set was not thought through is the
task's defect, and the reader should not pay for it.
