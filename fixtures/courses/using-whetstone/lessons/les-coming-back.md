Getting something wrong leaves one trace, and it is a plain one.

The course page carries a **Missed** list: the questions you got wrong and have not since
got right. Get one right and it leaves the list. There is no schedule, no due date, no
counter on the home screen, and nothing turns red because you have not been back in a week.

That is a deliberate omission rather than an unfinished feature. A queue with a due date on
it turns studying into homework you are behind on, and people close apps that make them feel
behind.

**Review** draws a handful of questions at random from what you have already covered. At
random is the whole of it: no weighting by how badly something went, no interval, no
estimate of what you are due. It is offline and costs nothing.

## When the question is the problem

Sometimes a question is not hard, it is broken: it rests on something untrue, it cannot be
done as written, or something in it fell over. Every question carries a small flag at the
end of its row for exactly that.

Reporting one is not an appeal against your mark, and it never changes a result. What
happens is that the model that wrote the course reads the question and your note, and either
agrees with you or names something you may have missed. **You can overrule it.** If the
report stands, your attempts at that question are voided and the question is mended or
removed.

:::callout{kind=insight}
Voided is not the same as failed and not the same as deleted. The attempt stays in the
record with its outcome taken off, so a broken question stops counting against you without
anything being erased.
:::

The same flag sits beside every module heading on the course page, for when the trouble is
bigger than one question and a whole module needs rebuilding or removing.
