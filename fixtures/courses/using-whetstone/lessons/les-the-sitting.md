A Test does not work like the questions in a Lesson, and the difference is the point of it.

You answer a question and press **Check**. The app works out how it went, writes the Attempt
down, and tells you nothing. The result is **held**. The line under the title counts how many
you have checked and says nothing about how any of them went.

When you check the last question in the Test, everything is revealed at once: the list of
questions with a tick or a cross beside each.

## Why it waits

Because the alternative changes how you answer. Told after question one that you were wrong,
you approach question two differently, and if a wrong answer offers another go, a
multiple-choice question stops being a question and becomes a process of elimination.

Immediate feedback is genuinely valuable, and this app has a place for it: a Try, in a
Lesson, right after the thing it checks. That leaves a Test free to be the other thing,
which is finding out what you can do unaided.

:::callout{kind=warning}
There is no mark at the top of the reveal. Not a score, not "7 of 9", not a percentage. If
you ever see one, something has gone wrong with the app rather than with you.
:::

## After the reveal

Two things follow it.

**Get feedback** goes through the questions one at a time: what you gave, whether it passed,
and what the course has to say about it.

**Retake** starts the sitting again with nothing shown. Both sittings stay in the record.
The first one is not deleted to make the second look better, because an attempt that
happened is a fact, and a record that quietly tidies itself is a record that lies.

Your answers are saved as you go, so a test you are halfway through survives closing the
window. Come back tomorrow and what you typed is still in the boxes.
