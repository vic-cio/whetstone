You are on a Page. Everything you read in Whetstone is one, and there are exactly two
kinds: a **Lesson**, which is this, and a **Test**, which is the other one.

The difference is not difficulty. It is what gets written down.

A Lesson records nothing. It can ask you as many questions as it likes, and none of them
goes anywhere. Answer one wrong, close the app, come back, and there is no trace of it. That
is deliberate: the moment reading is measured, people stop reading things they might get
wrong, and those are the things worth reading.

A Test records every answer. Each one becomes an **Attempt**: which question, which
objective, when, and how it went.

:::callout{kind=insight}
A question inside a Lesson is called a **Try**. The same question inside a Test is called a
**Task**. Identical on screen, and only one of them is ever recorded.
:::

## The tick

Beside every Page on the course page is one tickbox, and it is the only progress this app
keeps. There is no score, no percentage, no streak, and no estimate of how well you know
anything.

A Lesson ticks itself when you reach the end. A Test ticks once you have attempted every
question in it, whether you got them right or not, because the tick counts what you have
been through rather than how it went.

You can also tick anything by hand, or untick it. Ticking a Lesson you already know keeps
the course honest without deleting it, and the app never overrules you: read a Lesson again
after unticking it and it stays unticked.

## Moving around

The rail on the left holds one level at a time: your courses at home, and the pages of a
course once you are inside one. There is no breadcrumb, because there is nowhere deeper to
go.

The arrow keys walk the pages in order and escape goes back to the contents. Nothing is
locked and nothing is a gate: open any page in any order. The "next up" line at the foot of
a page is a suggestion from whoever wrote the course, and that is all it is.
